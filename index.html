Shamet off the Bench

Welcome to Shamet off the Bench! This is my fun and unserious NBA season preview that I write each season. This project all started in the summer of 2023 when I declared that the Wizards would make the playoffs, and that they even had "Shamet off the Bench". The Wizards ended up 15-67 that season and I was thoroughly mocked by my family members, but Landry Shamet is now a legend in the Irving family and the inspiration for my yearly write-up. I put progressively more time into writing each year, so I hope you enjoy!
